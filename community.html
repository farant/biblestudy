A Beautiful Passage from St. John Chrysostom

Found this while reading through the commentary on Matthew 5. Had to share it:

"The waters of baptism avail not for salvation, without the confession of faith; and this is a great proof of the divinity of the Spirit, since the faith in the Trinity is that which saves."
— St. John Chrysostom
