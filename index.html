Catena Aurea

Welcome to the reading group of St. Joseph's Church. Together we study the Catena Aurea—the "Golden Chain"—St. Thomas Aquinas's magnificent compilation of Church Father commentaries on the four Gospels.

Through the wisdom of the Fathers, we seek to deepen our understanding of Sacred Scripture and draw closer to Christ.

"And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself." — Luke 24:27
