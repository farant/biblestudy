Treasured Quotes

Favorite passages from our reading, shared by group members

✎

As we read through the Catena Aurea together, certain passages strike us with their beauty, clarity, or depth. This page collects those treasured quotes—from the Church Fathers, from St. Thomas himself, and from the Sacred Scriptures they illuminate.

Want to share a quote? Send it to Amanda or Fran and they will add it here, or submit it through our group.

✦

From the Church Fathers

For the Son of God became man, that He might make us gods. He was born of a woman, that we might be born of God.
— St. Augustine of Hippo
Shared by the group
Ignorance of Scripture is ignorance of Christ.
— St. Jerome
Shared by the group
The waters of baptism avail not for salvation, without the confession of faith; and this is a great proof of the divinity of the Spirit, since the faith in the Trinity is that which saves.
— St. John Chrysostom, Commentary on Matthew 28:19
Shared by the group

From the Catena Aurea

GLOSS. The whole life of Christ on earth, by the Man which He deigned to assume, was a system of moral teaching.
— Catena Aurea, Commentary on Matthew, Chapter 1
Shared by the group

From Sacred Scripture

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.
— Psalm 119:105
Shared by the group
All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness.
— 2 Timothy 3:16
Shared by the group
